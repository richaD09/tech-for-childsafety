INDIA · EMERGENCY SUPPORT

Need urgent help?

If you or a child is in immediate danger, move to a safe place and contact emergency services or a trusted adult.

What can you do?

01

Tell a trusted adult

Speak with a parent, teacher, guardian or another responsible adult you trust.

02

Keep evidence if safe

Save messages, screenshots or relevant details. Do not put yourself at risk to collect evidence.

03

Block and report

Use the platform's reporting and blocking features when you receive harmful messages or unwanted contact.

04

Seek official support

Contact an appropriate official child protection or emergency service when necessary.

It is never your fault when someone harms or threatens you.

You deserve to be listened to, believed and protected.
