ABOUT CHILDSAFE INDIA

Awareness today.
Safer tomorrow.

A student initiative exploring how technology and education can support child safety.

OUR PURPOSE

Technology can be a tool for protection.

Children today learn, connect and explore through technology. Alongside its opportunities, the digital world can bring risks that children and families need to understand. ChildSafe India was created to bring useful safety information together in a simple, accessible format.

01 / MISSION

Make awareness accessible.

Help children, parents and teachers understand safety risks and responsible technology use through clear information.

02 / VISION

Safer childhoods for all.

Encourage communities where every child can learn, grow, play and seek help without fear.

03 / APPROACH

Learn. Prevent. Protect.

Use education, digital literacy and community awareness to promote safer choices and help-seeking.

A project with a purpose.

ChildSafe India is a college educational project created to demonstrate how web technologies can support awareness about child protection. It is not a registered NGO, government platform or emergency service.
