FOR PARENTS & GUARDIANS

Guide with care.
Protect with trust.

Children are safer when they know they can talk to you about anything. Build trust before fear.

OPEN CONVERSATIONS

Make safety a conversation, not a restriction.

Children need guidance, boundaries and the confidence to ask for help without fear of punishment.

  • Listen without judging.
  • Ask what they enjoy online.
  • Discuss uncomfortable situations calmly.
  • Make sure they know they can always come to you.
PRACTICAL GUIDANCE

Five habits worth building.

01

Keep communication open

Talk regularly about the websites, apps and games your child enjoys. Make it easy for them to share worries.

02

Teach privacy

Explain why addresses, phone numbers, passwords, school details and OTPs must not be shared with strangers.

03

Understand the apps

Learn about the apps and online games your child uses. Review privacy settings together.

04

Set healthy boundaries

Agree on age-appropriate screen-time routines, device-free times and safe online behaviour.

05

Respond with support

If your child shares an upsetting experience, remain calm, listen, reassure them and seek appropriate help.

Children need support, not blame.

If a child reports an online incident, listen carefully and reassure them. Focus on their safety and the next steps.
