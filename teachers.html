FOR TEACHERS & EDUCATORS

A safer classroom
starts with listening.

Schools play a powerful role in helping children feel safe, respected and confident enough to speak up.

AWARENESS IN EDUCATION

Every child deserves a trusted adult.

Safe learning environments are built through respect, communication, awareness and consistent support.

  • Encourage students to ask for help.
  • Teach online privacy and digital responsibility.
  • Address bullying and exclusion.
  • Follow school safeguarding procedures.
CLASSROOM PRACTICES

Make safety part of everyday learning.

01

Digital awareness

Teach students to recognise unsafe links, protect personal details and use digital platforms responsibly.

02

Bullying prevention

Promote kindness, inclusion and respectful communication. Address bullying consistently.

03

Listen and reassure

When a student shares a concern, listen calmly without blame or pressure.

04

Recognise concerns

Pay attention to changes in behaviour, distress, withdrawal or reports of threats.

05

Follow safeguarding rules

Refer concerns through the appropriate school safeguarding procedures and official services.

Awareness can make a difference.

Use classroom conversations, activities and digital safety lessons to help children recognise risks and seek help.
