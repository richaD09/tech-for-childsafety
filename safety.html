DIGITAL WELLBEING

Navigate the internet.
Stay safe. Stay smart.

Technology opens doors to learning and creativity. Knowing how to use it safely makes the journey better.

01

Cyberbullying

Cyberbullying means hurting, threatening or embarrassing someone through digital platforms.

What you can do

  • Don't retaliate against threatening messages.
  • Save evidence if safe.
  • Block and report the account.
  • Tell a trusted adult.
02

Protect your privacy

Your personal information belongs to you. Think carefully before sharing it online.

Keep private

  • Home address and phone number
  • Passwords and OTPs
  • School details with strangers
  • Private photographs and live location
03

Online strangers

People online may not be who they claim to be. Be careful with new contacts.

Remember

  • Don't agree to secret meetings.
  • Don't send photos under pressure.
  • Don't keep uncomfortable requests secret.
  • Ask a trusted adult for help.
04

Social media

Use social media in ways that protect your wellbeing and personal information.

Smart habits

  • Use privacy settings.
  • Think before posting.
  • Accept requests only from people you know.
  • Report harmful content.
05

Gaming safety

Online games can be fun, but chat features may connect you with strangers.

Stay safe

  • Never share personal details in game chat.
  • Avoid suspicious links.
  • Block users who make you uncomfortable.
  • Tell an adult if something feels wrong.
06

Password safety

A strong password makes it harder for others to access your accounts.

Protect your accounts

  • Use long, unique passwords.
  • Never share your password or OTP.
  • Enable two-factor authentication when available.
  • Ask an adult for help securing accounts.

Something online doesn't feel right?

You are not at fault for someone else's harmful behaviour. Talk to someone you trust and get help.
